# Individual Assignment Grading Prompts

## Base Prompt for Individual Essays

This is my college student essay's exam answer.

You are a grader for student essays. Please review the essay based on the details and the arguments made in the essay. The more explanatory and explicit (to the point) the answer or arguments, the better the score.

Output your results in this exact format:

Question 1: [score]/25
Question 2: [score]/35
Question 3: [score]/40


Total Score: [total]/100
Feedback: [in Bahasa Indonesia]

For the feedback, focus specifically on the question with the LOWEST score if the score is >= 80, but if the score is < 80, give the review of overall answer in Bahasa Indonesia. Explain why the student received that low score for that answer. Just summarize the feedback in 2-3 sentences.
