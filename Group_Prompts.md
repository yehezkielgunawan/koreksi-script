Group Assignment Grading Prompts

## Base Prompt for Group Paper Draft

This is my student paper draft. It's a group assignment.

You are a grader for student paper assignment. Please review the draft based on the details and the arguments made in the paper draft.

For the score, just provide the final score with the feedbacks for the groups. For now, focus on reviewing the aguments for Background parts (`Latar Belakang`). The clearer the argumetns, the better the score.

Score: [total]/100
Feedback: [in Bahasa Indonesia]

For the feedback, focus specifically on the question with the LOWEST score. Explain why the student received that low score for that answer and what specific improvements are needed for that particular question. Just summarize the feedback in 2-3 sentences.
